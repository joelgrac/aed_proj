Project developed for the Algorithms and Data Structures course, consisting of a C implementation of a navigation system on a grid. In Fase 1, basic functionalities were implemented, such as identifying the best energy cell within k steps, calculating energy sums, and generating simple paths. Fase 2 introduced more advanced search algorithms, focusing on optimizing the final energy after exactly k steps and reaching a target energy amount without depleting the reserve. Feat Filipa Cunha
